I'm a product manager who works at the intersection of fintech, entrepreneurship, and product strategy. I've taken a platform from 0→1 to 7,000+ users as a founder, guided 100+ early-stage founders through discovery, MVP design, and go-to-market, and worked inside regulated payments and lending operations at Neo Financial and Quantify.

That range means I speak both the language of users and the language of the business. I've written the PRD at midnight and shipped it in the morning, turned business requirements into specs engineering can build, and made hard roadmap trade-offs under real resource constraints. I've also sat across the table from investors, founders, and operators working out product-market fit under pressure.

I'm especially focused on AI and the practical use of LLMs to build better user experiences. I built a grant-matching tool on the Claude API that helped 50+ founders secure funding. My MBA at Schulich sharpened the strategy; years of shipping and advising built the product instincts.
